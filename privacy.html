Visitor statistics

The public homepage and interactive results page use GoatCounter to count pageviews and estimate visitors. The statistics dashboard is private; no visitor counter is displayed on this website.

GoatCounter does not set visitor tracking cookies. It temporarily processes network information to distinguish repeat visits; see its privacy policy for details. Our account stores aggregate counts, with individual-pageview records and browser, device, location, language and referrer statistics disabled.

Our integration excludes URL query strings and section links, sends no referring-page address, and does not record gallery selections or clicks. It respects browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control settings. Local previews and this privacy page do not load analytics.
