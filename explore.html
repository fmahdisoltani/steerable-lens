04 / WHAT THE MEASUREMENTS MEAN

Keep the questions separate.

Steerable Lens is a controlled instrument for probing sensitivity. Each measurement answers a different question about the resulting edit.

G / RESPONSE

Did the optimized score rise?

The driver classifier supplies the gradient. Its target probability measures the objective’s response, so it is not independent evidence that the desired visual change occurred.

O, O₂ / TRANSFER

Do other classifiers agree?

Two judges are never differentiated through during editing. Their target predictions measure transfer. Agreement does not establish realism, identity preservation, or human recognition.

E / RECONSTRUCTION

Does a smooth transformation fit?

A classifier-free inverse warp, with optional spatial gain, approximates the edit. E measures the fraction of edit energy recovered by this restricted family.

Three editing regimes, with different coordinate anchors

Frozen sensitivity: one gradient at the source, followed by a finite extrapolation. Persistent coordinates: refreshed gradients update increments anchored to the source decomposition; this is used for the matched MNIST and CelebA comparisons. Step-local steering: reanalysis of the current image at every step; amplitudes and residuals can drift across the trajectory. The natural-image playback above uses this last regime.

Limits on interpretation and generalization

MNIST test images and CelebA faces were used in earlier experiments, and the legacy O checkpoint was selected on test performance. These runs are retrospective evidence, not fresh held-out confirmation. CelebA reconstruction estimates are conditional on common budget attainment; the lowest coverage is 7/25 for smile addition. Fits are restricted and nonconvex. Selected examples illustrate outcomes rather than estimate their frequency. The human pilot is small, and personal familiarity with the researchers may influence responses.

How the images and measurements on this page were prepared

Example images and trajectory frames are exported from saved arrays with only conversion to an 8-bit PNG. MNIST is displayed with nearest-neighbor scaling. Chart values are recomputed from the saved fits on common sources, and G/O scores come from saved endpoint records. Scores are rounded for display; the download retains their full precision. The first four MNIST cases and Eyeglasses_183143 were chosen after inspecting outcomes. The other MNIST case is first in record order; remaining CelebA cases are the first common-attainment IDs in sorted order.
